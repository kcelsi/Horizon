## [Artificial Neural Networks Self-Assemble Learning Machinery](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1x6hmyg/how_artificial_neural_networks_build_their_own/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have demonstrated that artificial neural networks, governed by local plasticity rules, can self-assemble learning motifs and achieve performance comparable to traditional backpropagation methods in tasks like pattern recognition and image classification. This breakthrough suggests a novel approach to designing neural networks that mimics biological synapse assembly, potentially leading to more biologically plausible and efficient AI systems. The study highlights that randomly interconnected neuron-like units can organize themselves into learning circuits based on shared local plasticity rules, distinguishing this from directly designing a learning circuit.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Dorian182 · Oct 15, 10:01

**Background**: Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are computational models inspired by the structure and function of biological neural networks. Backpropagation is a widely used algorithm for training ANNs, which efficiently computes gradients to update network weights. Local plasticity rules, inspired by biological synapses, describe how connections between neurons change based on local activity.

**Discussion**: The Reddit discussion highlights the novelty of self-organization in ANNs and its potential implications for understanding biological learning, while also noting the authors' caution against directly equating this to brain mechanisms and its current limitations for large-scale models.
