## [Nvidia Launches DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering](https://t.me/zaihuapd/44389) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Nvidia has officially launched DLSS 5, introducing 3D-guided neural rendering for more realistic lighting and materials in real-time graphics. This new technology will debut on September 3rd with the release of NBA 2K27. DLSS 5 represents a significant advancement in AI-powered graphics rendering, promising to bridge the gap between real-time game visuals and Hollywood-level CGI. This will enhance the gaming experience for users with compatible hardware and potentially influence future visual computing applications. DLSS 5 is available for GeForce RTX 50 series GPUs and GeForce NOW Ultimate members, with performance benchmarks showing up to 370 FPS at 4K with ray tracing on an RTX 5090. The technology utilizes a one-step pixel-space diffusion model for high-resolution, real-time rendering.

It works by inferring detail from a lower-resolution render, allowing for higher frame rates or graphical settings. DLSS 5 builds upon this by incorporating neural rendering techniques.

## [Apple Silicon Devices Power a Dynamic Distributed Virtual GPU Cloud](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/distributed-gpu-cloud) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have proposed a novel system that creates a dynamic and distributed virtual GPU cloud by utilizing idle Apple Silicon devices for machine learning inference tasks. This approach could democratize access to powerful computing resources for AI inference by leveraging existing, underutilized hardware, potentially reducing costs and increasing efficiency for businesses and researchers. The system dynamically distributes inference workloads across a network of Apple Silicon Macs, aiming to provide a cost-effective alternative to traditional cloud GPU services for non-real-time tasks.

## [Judge Orders Google to Simplify Third-Party App Store Installations](https://t.me/zaihuapd/44392) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A US district judge has ordered Google to remove obstacles for installing third-party app stores on Android within one week, eliminating extra warning steps and pop-ups that were deemed anti-competitive friction. This ruling directly impacts Google's control over app distribution on Android, potentially fostering greater competition and offering users more choices beyond the Play Store. The judge found that multi-step processes, like requiring users to 'view' before 'installing,' were intentionally created 'anti-competitive friction' to deter average users, and mandated that installation should be as straightforward as installing any other Android app.

**Background**: This order stems from the Epic Games v. Google antitrust lawsuit, where a jury previously found Google illegally monopolized Android app distribution. Anti-competitive practices are business actions that hinder market competition, often regulated by antitrust laws to protect smaller businesses and consumers.

Google has launched the Gemma 4 12B, a new open-source large language model positioned between its mobile-friendly and professional versions. This model requires approximately 16GB of system memory or VRAM, making it operable on standard laptops. The release of Gemma 4 12B democratizes access to powerful AI capabilities, enabling developers and researchers with standard hardware to experiment with and deploy advanced LLMs. This move aligns with the trend of making AI more accessible and fosters broader innovation within the open-source community. Google states that Gemma 4 12B achieves benchmark performance close to its larger 26B MoE version, despite using about half the memory. The Gemma 4 series, now under the Apache 2.0 license, includes multimodal capabilities for text and image input.

Mixture of Experts (MoE) is an architecture where different parts of the model specialize in different tasks, potentially leading to more efficient computation. Open-source models allow public access to model weights and code, fostering collaboration and customization.
