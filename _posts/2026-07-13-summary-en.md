This system is designed to restore hand grasping function for patients with spinal cord injuries. This approval represents a significant milestone in assistive technology and neuroscience, offering a new hope for individuals with severe mobility impairments. It paves the way for broader clinical adoption of invasive BCI technologies, potentially transforming rehabilitation and quality of life for many. The device utilizes epidural micro-implantation and wireless power and communication technology, and is intended for patients aged 18-60 with tetraplegia due to cervical spinal cord injury. Clinical trials demonstrated significant improvements in hand grasping ability and quality of life for participants.

## [Chromium's Math.tanh function reveals OS-level browser fingerprinting](https://scrapfly.dev/posts/browser-math-os-fingerprint/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Since Chromium 148, the behavior of the Math.tanh function has been observed to differ across operating systems, enabling a new method for browser fingerprinting that can identify the underlying OS. This discovery introduces a novel privacy risk, as it allows websites to potentially identify users' operating systems through a seemingly innocuous JavaScript function, impacting user privacy and security. The fingerprinting relies on subtle variations in the floating-point results of Math.tanh for specific inputs, which are influenced by the underlying operating system's implementation of mathematical functions.

**Background**: Browser fingerprinting is a technique used to collect information about a device's software and hardware to create a unique identifier, often used for tracking users across websites even without cookies. Hyperbolic functions, like tanh, are mathematical functions analogous to trigonometric functions, defined using the hyperbola.

## [Zer0Fit: Local Server for Google's TabFM & TimesFM Models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1uue8cc/zer0fit_i_took_googles_new_tabfm_timesfm_ml/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A graduate student has developed Zer0Fit, a local Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that integrates Google's TabFM and TimesFM foundation models, enabling zero-shot machine learning tasks like forecasting, classification, and regression. This project democratizes access to advanced foundation models for tabular and time-series data, significantly lowering the barrier for users to perform complex ML tasks locally without extensive training or tuning. Zer0Fit requires 16GB of VRAM, runs on PyTorch (CUDA only), and features dynamic model loading/unloading with a 5-minute TTL to manage VRAM usage, supporting CSV input with plans for more formats.

**Background**: TabFM is a foundation model from Google Research for tabular data classification and regression, designed to perform tasks zero-shot by framing them as in-context learning problems. TimesFM is a similar decoder-only foundation model from Google Research specifically for time-series forecasting, pre-trained on extensive real-world time-point data.
