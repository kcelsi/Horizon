## [EDG C++ Front-End Open-Sourced Under Apache-2.0 with LLVM-Exception](https://edgcpp.org/#transition) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The EDG C++ front-end, a historically significant component in C++ development tools, has been made open-source under an Apache-2.0 with LLVM-exception license on September 30, 2026, with The C++ Alliance becoming its new home. This move is significant for the C++ ecosystem as the EDG front-end has been a foundational component in numerous commercial compilers and tools, potentially enabling broader community contributions and innovation in C++ development. The EDG C++ front-end has a long history, with early commits dating back to 1990, and has been used by major industry players like Microsoft Visual C++ for IntelliSense, Intel C++ Compiler, and NVIDIA CUDA Compiler.

## [CO₂Jump Sampler Enhances Text-Image Consistency in Multimodal Generation](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1wtyl5m/concurrent_image_understanding_and_generation/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have introduced CO₂Jump, a novel sampler for concurrent image and text generation that improves consistency by using text confidence and cross-modal attention to guide image updates and allow for revision of earlier decisions. This development is significant for multimodal AI, as it addresses a key challenge of ensuring that generated images accurately reflect their corresponding textual descriptions, potentially leading to more reliable and coherent AI-generated content. CO₂Jump operates with one model forward pass per denoising step and requires no additional training, using text confidence and cross-modal attention to mask and regenerate low-confidence tokens, thereby enabling revision of earlier generation choices.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Upstairs_Theme2785 · Sep 30, 07:28

**Background**: Markov Jump Processes are a type of stochastic process where the state can change abruptly at random points in time. In the context of AI, particularly generative models, these processes can model transitions between different states of generation. Cross-modal attention is a mechanism that allows different sensory modalities, like text and images, to influence each other's processing, enabling better alignment and understanding between them.

## [Cloudflare Launches Public Certificate Authority for Enhanced Web Security](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-certificate-authority/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Cloudflare has announced its intention to become a public Certificate Authority (CA) and has applied to join major root certificate programs from Chrome, Apple, Microsoft, and Mozilla. The company has also signed an agreement to acquire a trusted root certificate from GlobalSign, though it has not yet begun issuing certificates. This move signifies Cloudflare's strategic expansion into web infrastructure services, potentially impacting the certificate authority market and enhancing overall web security. It also positions Cloudflare to support emerging internet standards like post-quantum cryptography. The new CA will prioritize support for the ACME protocol for automated certificate issuance and renewal, and plans to issue production-grade Merkle Tree Certificates (MTCs) by Q1 2027 to facilitate a post-quantum internet.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 30, 06:26

**Background**: A Certificate Authority (CA) is a trusted entity that issues digital certificates, which are essential for verifying the identity of websites and enabling secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS) via TLS/SSL. The ACME protocol automates the process of obtaining and renewing these certificates, making it more efficient. Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) refers to cryptographic algorithms designed to be secure against attacks from both classical and future quantum computers.

## [Google DeepMind adds watermarks to AI-designed proteins](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/09/google-figures-out-how-to-watermark-ai-designed-proteins/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google DeepMind has introduced SynthID Bio, a method to embed undetectable watermarks into AI-generated protein sequences, allowing for verification of origin and aiding in safety screening. This development is significant for biosecurity and scientific integrity, as it provides a way to distinguish between proteins designed by trusted AI models and those potentially created for malicious purposes. The watermarking process, integrated with tools like ProteinMPNN, aims to modify amino acid sequences only when it does not impact protein function, and experimental results show that watermarked proteins retain their binding capabilities.

**Background**: Proteins are essential molecules in biology, performing a vast array of functions. AI is increasingly being used to design novel proteins with specific properties.
