## [Global Solar Power Generation Surpasses Nuclear for the First Time](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/09/29/solar-surpasses-nuclear-power-globally-for-first-time/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

For the first time in history, global solar power generation has exceeded nuclear power generation. This milestone was achieved due to the rapid expansion of solar energy capacity worldwide. This event signifies a major shift in the global energy landscape, highlighting the accelerating transition towards renewable energy sources. It indicates that solar power is becoming a dominant force in electricity generation, impacting energy policies and investments. While solar generation has surpassed nuclear, nuclear power maintains a substantially higher capacity factor. However, solar's rapid growth has led to it generating more terawatt-hours overall, with wind power also nearing nuclear generation levels.

hackernews · xbmcuser · Oct 17, 14:01 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50131256)

**Background**: Capacity factor refers to the ratio of a power plant's actual output over a period to its potential output if it were operating at full nameplate capacity continuously. Solar power's capacity factor is typically lower than nuclear power's due to its intermittency (dependent on sunlight).

**Discussion**: Commenters noted that this achievement is based on actual generated terawatt-hours, not just capacity. There's discussion about nuclear power's stagnation, the need to accelerate clean energy further to replace all fossil fuels, and the potential for solar and wind to surpass hydroelectric power in the future.

## [Untethered Jailbreak for iPhone 18 Pro Demonstrated, First Since iOS 9](https://x.com/calif_io/status/2111080240978223545?s=20) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A full untethered jailbreak for the iPhone 18 Pro running iOS 27.0.1 has been publicly demonstrated by security researcher @calif_io. This jailbreak retains its state after reboots and does not require a computer for re-activation. This development is significant as it represents the first publicly demonstrated full untethered jailbreak for iOS since iOS 9, potentially impacting Apple's security ecosystem. It demonstrates that sophisticated attack chains can still bypass Apple's latest security measures. The exploit chain involves three layers: the cryptex subsystem, TSS ticket spoofing, and PAC (Pointer Authentication Codes) protection. This demonstration occurred shortly after Apple patched 273 vulnerabilities in a recent update.

**Background**: An untethered jailbreak allows an iOS device to remain jailbroken after a reboot without needing to be connected to a computer. This is in contrast to a tethered jailbreak, which requires a computer connection to re-activate the jailbreak after each reboot. Jailbreaking involves bypassing Apple's software restrictions to install unauthorized apps and modifications.

## [China Mobile and Qualcomm Test World's First 6G in U6G Band](https://t.me/zaihuapd/44485) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On September 20th, China Mobile and Qualcomm successfully completed the world's first 6G connection test in the U6G frequency band at the China Mobile Collaborative Innovation Base. This test utilized a prototype base station and terminal prototype, covering a massive channel bandwidth of 400 MHz for downlink and 200 MHz for uplink, along with 128-channel massive MIMO. This milestone validates the feasibility of future 6G network, terminal, and service co-evolution, demonstrating significant progress in developing next-generation wireless communication technologies. It signals a step forward for the global telecommunications industry towards higher bandwidth and more advanced wireless capabilities. The test integrated base station and terminal prototypes into a single end-to-end link, validating the potential for future 6G networks. The U6G band, part of 3GPP's extended FR1 range, supports substantial bandwidths crucial for advanced wireless applications.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 17, 13:02

**Background**: 3GPP (3rd Generation Partnership Project) is the standards organization responsible for developing protocols for mobile telecommunications, including 5G NR and future 6G standards. MIMO (Multiple-Input Multiple-Output) is a wireless technology that uses multiple antennas at both the transmitter and receiver to increase data throughput and link capacity. The U6G frequency band is part of the broader spectrum being considered for future wireless generations, extending the capabilities of existing 5G frequency ranges.

OpenCode confirmed a large-scale distillation attack resulted in the exposure of API keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google stored by approximately 1.4% of its Console users. Affected users have been notified via email. This incident highlights significant security vulnerabilities in how AI service API keys are stored and managed, potentially impacting the integrity and cost of AI services for many users and underscoring the need for robust security practices across the AI ecosystem. OpenCode is collaborating with the three AI vendors to investigate potential misuse of the compromised keys, and recommends affected users revoke and re-sign their keys, monitor usage since October 17th, and set spending limits.
