## [ASML CEO: No reason to deny service for existing China equipment sales](https://t.me/zaihuapd/45387) ⭐️ 8.0/10

ASML CEO Peter Wennink stated on January 17th that there is currently no reason not to service lithography equipment already sold to Chinese customers, amidst ongoing negotiations between the US and Dutch governments regarding export restrictions. This statement from ASML's CEO highlights the potential impact of geopolitical tensions on the global semiconductor supply chain, as ASML is a critical supplier of advanced manufacturing equipment. ASML's Chinese market demand is strong, currently accounting for 20% of its total orders, with China being its third-largest market after Taiwan and South Korea.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 12:19

**Background**: ASML is a Dutch multinational technology company that manufactures photolithography machines essential for producing integrated circuits. Lithography is a process used in semiconductor manufacturing to create intricate patterns on silicon wafers. ASML is the world's only commercial manufacturer of extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines, crucial for producing the most advanced chips.

## [Nvidia Halts AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Nations Due to US Export Controls](https://t.me/zaihuapd/45392) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Nvidia has instructed its major manufacturing partners to stop shipping AI servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia, impacting companies like Dell and Supermicro. This move directly affects the supply chain for advanced AI hardware, potentially hindering AI development in these regions and impacting cloud service providers that rely on these components. The halt is linked to new US export control regulations requiring regional cloud service providers to obtain export licenses and verify customers are not restricted Chinese entities within 90 days.

telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 26, 15:14

**Background**: These four Southeast Asian countries have served as transit points for Chinese AI companies seeking advanced Nvidia processors, which are restricted from direct export to mainland China. The US government has been increasingly implementing export controls to manage national security and trade competition with China, particularly concerning advanced AI chips.
