Artificial intelligence is demonstrating a growing proficiency in discovering counterexamples to mathematical conjectures, a task traditionally performed by human mathematicians. It may free up human mathematicians to focus on more creative and abstract aspects of the field, while AI handles the exhaustive search for counterexamples. Formal verification uses mathematical methods to prove or disprove the correctness of systems or statements.

This vulnerability is significant because Fastjson 1.x is no longer maintained, meaning an official patch is improbable, forcing users to migrate to Fastjson2 or implement workarounds to avoid potential exploitation. The vulnerability can be exploited on various JDK versions without requiring specific configurations like autoTypeSupport or the presence of classpath gadgets, and the only recommended solutions are upgrading to Fastjson2 or enabling SafeMode.
