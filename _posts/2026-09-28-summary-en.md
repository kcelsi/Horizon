## [China's Data Center Capacity Surpasses Europe and Asia Combined for AI](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-chinese-ai-infrastructure-boom) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China's delivered data center capacity has exceeded 24GW, surpassing the combined total of EMEA and the rest of Asia, according to SemiAnalysis. Existing retail data halls are being rapidly upgraded with high-density electrical and liquid cooling to support AI clusters, creating a massive physical computing power pool second only to North America. This massive expansion signifies a significant shift in global AI infrastructure, with Chinese tech giants aggressively investing to secure computing power, leading to unprecedented capital expenditures and impacting their free cash flow. This arms race highlights the intense competition and substantial financial commitment required for AI development. ByteDance alone accounts for nearly 20% of China's delivered capacity and has set a record for deploying 100MW in 12 months in core regions. Alibaba, Tencent, and Baidu's combined capital expenditure for Q2 2026 is projected to reach $20 billion, doubling year-over-year, with all three historically recording negative free cash flow for the first time.

**Background**: Data centers are facilities that house computing systems and associated components, such as telecommunications and storage systems. High-density electrical refers to systems designed to handle a large amount of electrical current within a small area, crucial for powering intensive AI hardware. Liquid cooling leverages fluids to transfer heat more efficiently than air, essential for managing the thermal output of high-performance computing.
