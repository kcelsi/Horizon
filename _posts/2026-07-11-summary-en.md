## [China's Long March 10B Achieves World's First Rocket Booster Net Recovery](https://weibo.com/7340734455/R814of1Ki) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On July 10, China's Long March 10B (长征十号乙) launch vehicle successfully lifted off from the Hainan commercial space launch site. Approximately six minutes after first and second stage separation, the first stage booster returned vertically and was successfully recovered using a net system on a sea platform. This marks a significant advancement in reusable rocket technology, as it is the world's first successful recovery of a rocket booster using a net system. This achievement lays a solid foundation for low-cost, large-scale access to space. The net recovery system utilizes a grid of cables on a recovery ship to catch the booster's hooks, providing cushioning and braking without the need for landing legs. This method enables the undamaged recovery and reuse of the first stage.
