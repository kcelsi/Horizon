## [Ant Group's Lingbo Open-Sources LingBot-Video, First MoE Embodied Video Foundation Model](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/07/446458.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Ant Group's Lingbo has open-sourced LingBot-Video, the world's first embodied video foundation model utilizing a Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture. The model boasts 30 billion parameters but activates only around 3 billion during inference, offering approximately three times the efficiency of dense models of similar scale. This release is significant as it introduces a novel MoE architecture to embodied AI video generation, pushing the boundaries of robotics research and practical applications. Its superior performance on robotics benchmarks and high inference efficiency could accelerate the development and deployment of more capable AI agents in real-world scenarios. LingBot-Video innovates through its DiT+MoE design, a curated 70,000-hour embodied dataset covering diverse robotic interactions, and a multi-dimensional reinforcement learning reward system emphasizing physical plausibility and task completion. It is released under the Apache 2.0 license on GitHub.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 9, 04:30

**Background**: Mixture of Experts (MoE) is an architectural approach in neural networks where multiple 'expert' sub-networks are trained, and a gating mechanism routes input to specific experts. This allows models to scale to very large parameter counts while keeping inference costs manageable, as only a subset of parameters are activated per input. Embodied AI refers to AI systems that can perceive, reason, and act within a physical or simulated environment, often involving robotics.
