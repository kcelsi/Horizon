## [New Functional Gradient Descent with Adaptive Representations Guarantees Global Convergence](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1wsejb7/functional_gradient_descent_with_adaptive/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have developed a novel method for Functional Gradient Descent utilizing adaptive representations, which has been accepted at NeurIPS and provably ensures convergence to the global minimizer. This breakthrough addresses a key challenge in functional gradient descent by enabling accurate implementation of infinite-dimensional gradients, potentially leading to algorithms that significantly outperform current neural networks in various settings. The method formalizes a broad class of approximation schemes termed 'adaptive representations' that, unlike naive approximations, provably ensure convergence to the global minimizer and are immediately implementable.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/dccsillag0 · Sep 28, 13:23

**Background**: Functional Gradient Descent (FGD) is an optimization technique used when the objective function is itself a function, often operating in infinite-dimensional spaces. Unlike standard gradient descent which operates on vectors in R^n, FGD operates on functions. A key challenge in FGD is approximating the infinite-dimensional functional gradient, as naive approximations can lead to convergence to incorrect solutions.

## [Space-Based Laser Power Transmission Set for First Orbital Test](https://www.wired.com/story/space-lasers-are-about-to-get-their-first-real-test-generating-energy/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

US startup Star Catcher plans to conduct the first orbital test of space-based laser power transmission, beaming energy from one satellite to another using a SpaceX rocket. This test aims to demonstrate the ability to transmit power wirelessly between two independent spacecraft in orbit. This orbital test is significant as it could pave the way for a future space-based energy grid, potentially reducing satellite reliance on large batteries and enabling high-power operations for future infrastructure like space data centers. The proposed system involves an 'energy node' satellite focusing sunlight and converting it into a laser beam to power the solar panels of other satellites. Star Catcher, founded in 2024, claims to hold a world record for optical power beaming and plans to bring its technology to orbit in 2026.

This milestone marks a significant step for SpaceX's Starship program, demonstrating its capability for orbital flight and satellite deployment, which is crucial for future space exploration missions, including NASA's Artemis program. Although an engine shut down prematurely, the flight team successfully achieved orbit and decided to end the mission early, with the spacecraft splashing down north of Hawaii; the exact reason for the early return was not disclosed by SpaceX.
