## [Mojo programming language is now open source](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/18/mojo-is-now-open-source/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The Mojo programming language, developed by Modular Inc., has been officially released as open source under the Apache 2 license. This follows the recent release of Mojo 1.0 and fulfills a promise made in May 2023. This move makes Mojo, a language designed for high-performance AI development and heterogeneous hardware, accessible to the broader community. Its open-source nature is expected to accelerate adoption and ecosystem growth, potentially impacting the AI/ML development landscape. Mojo's compiler and toolchain are now available under the Apache 2 license. While initially envisioned as a Python superset, the project's direction has shifted, and it is now considered its own language optimized for AI and GPU programming, inspired by Python syntax.

**Background**: Mojo is a systems programming language that builds on the MLIR compiler framework, allowing it to target various hardware like CPUs and GPUs efficiently. It aims to combine Python's user-friendliness with the performance of languages like Rust, making it particularly well-suited for AI applications.

## [Framework Laptop Repaired After Bricking Incident with AMD 7040 Series CPU](https://quantum5.ca/2026/08/16/fixing-bricked-amd-7040-series-framework-13-laptop-with-20-tools/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A user successfully revived a bricked Framework laptop equipped with an AMD 7040 series CPU by following a detailed guide and utilizing approximately 20 tools, including pogo pins for BIOS flashing. This incident highlights a critical failure point in modern electronics, where faulty updates can render expensive devices unusable, raising questions about manufacturer responsibility and consumer rights for repairability. The repair involved using pogo pins to access the BIOS chip externally, as the Framework laptop's motherboard did not have a populated header for flashing due to cost considerations.

The AMD 7040 series is a line of mobile processors featuring Zen 4 architecture and RDNA 3 integrated graphics.

## [China's Long March 10B achieves world's first rocket stage net recovery](https://t.me/zaihuapd/43264) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 10th, the Long March 10B carrier rocket successfully launched from the Hainan commercial space launch site and completed the world's first net recovery of its first stage after separation. This successful net recovery marks a significant advancement in rocket reusability technology, potentially reducing launch costs and increasing the frequency of space missions. The first stage was vertically recovered on a maritime platform approximately six minutes after stage separation, representing China's first controlled recovery of a carrier rocket's first stage.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 19, 00:16

**Background**: Rocket reusability is a key trend in the space industry aimed at lowering the cost of space access. Traditional recovery methods include landing legs or 'chopsticks' systems. Net recovery, as demonstrated by China, offers an alternative approach by using a large net to catch the descending stage.
