## [HBM Memory Chips Surpass Leading-Edge Logic Chips in Value Per Area](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/dram/dram-is-now-more-expensive-than-compute-chips-on-per-area-basis-ai-demand-drives-memory-die-value-past-leading-edge-silicon) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Driven by escalating AI infrastructure demands, High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) chips have now surpassed leading-edge logic chips in value per unit area. This shift underscores the growing importance of memory manufacturers within the AI supply chain. This development signifies a major shift in the semiconductor industry, where memory components are becoming as, or even more, critical than processing units for AI workloads. It highlights the strategic importance of memory supply for the future of AI development and deployment. The increased value of HBM per unit area is attributed to its complex manufacturing processes, including advanced stacking, sophisticated packaging, and stringent yield controls, all necessary to meet the high bandwidth and capacity demands of AI accelerators.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 23, 11:39

**Background**: High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is a type of high-performance RAM designed for applications requiring massive data throughput, such as AI and high-performance computing. Unlike traditional memory, HBM utilizes a 2.5D or 3D architecture with an exceptionally wide data path, enabling significantly higher bandwidth and lower power consumption in a smaller form factor. Leading-edge logic chips refer to the most advanced processors and accelerators manufactured using the latest semiconductor fabrication processes.
