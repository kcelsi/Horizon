## [Atmosphere Detected on Earth-like Exoplanet in Habitable Zone](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy4kdd1e0ejo) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Astronomers have detected an atmosphere around the exoplanet LHS 1140b, which is an Earth-sized rocky planet located in the habitable zone of its star. This marks the first time an atmosphere has been confirmed on such a planet, a significant milestone in the search for extraterrestrial life. This discovery is crucial because it demonstrates that Earth-like planets in the habitable zones of stars can retain atmospheres, a key ingredient for potential habitability. It advances the search for life beyond Earth by providing a promising target for further study. The detection was made using the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) via emission spectroscopy, specifically by observing the planet as it passed behind its star. This method ruled out the possibility of LHS 1140b being a mini-Neptune, confirming its rocky nature and the presence of an atmosphere.

hackernews · neversaydie · Jul 17, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947560)

**Background**: An exoplanet is a planet that orbits a star outside our solar system. The habitable zone, often called the 'Goldilocks zone,' is the region around a star where temperatures are just right for liquid water to exist on a planet's surface, a condition considered essential for life as we know it. LHS 1140b orbits a red dwarf star, which is cooler and smaller than our Sun, meaning its habitable zone is closer to the star.

Huawei publicly showcased its Ascend 950 SuperPoD, a large-scale AI computing system, for the first time at the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC). This launch signifies a major advancement in AI hardware from a key global player, directly challenging Nvidia's dominance in the high-performance computing market. The Lingqu interconnect protocol is Huawei's proprietary technology designed to facilitate high-speed communication between a large number of computing units in AI systems.
