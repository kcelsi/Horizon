## [Asahi Linux Achieves Official Support for Apple's M3 Chips](https://asahilinux.org/2026/09/m2-episode-1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Asahi Linux project has announced official support for Apple's M3 series of chips, enabling Linux to run on the latest generation of Apple Silicon Macs. This expansion follows previous support for M1 and M2 chips. This milestone significantly broadens the usability of Linux on modern Apple hardware, offering users an alternative operating system for their powerful M3-based devices. It represents a major step forward in the open-source community's effort to support Apple's proprietary hardware. While official support for M3 is now available, some functionalities like HDMI output are still under development, posing a potential roadblock for widespread adoption. Performance comparisons, particularly with native applications like llama.cpp using Metal, are also a point of discussion.

**Background**: Asahi Linux is a project dedicated to porting the Linux kernel and associated software to Apple Silicon Macs, which use ARM-based system-on-a-chip (SoC) designs. Apple's M3 chips, released in late 2023, are the third generation of these custom processors, succeeding the M2 and built on a 3-nanometer process technology for improved performance and efficiency.

## [ML Research Reproducibility Declining Due to Hardware, Demos, and Corporate Secrecy](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1w92eis/reproducibility_seems_to_be_headed_towards/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A recent discussion on Reddit highlights concerns that reproducibility in machine learning research is becoming increasingly difficult. The author cites the prohibitive cost of specialized hardware, the unreliability of video demonstrations, and the lack of transparency from large AI companies as primary drivers of this trend. This decline in reproducibility threatens the integrity of scientific progress in AI, making it harder to verify claims and build upon existing work. It could lead to wasted resources and a slower pace of genuine innovation if results cannot be independently validated. Specific issues include the move towards 'physical AI' requiring expensive labs, the incentive for researchers and companies to only showcase successful demo aspects, and the difficulty of verifying claims made by large corporations due to vague problem definitions and proprietary data/code.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/NeighborhoodFatCat · Sep 6, 17:29

**Background**: Reproducibility in scientific research refers to the ability of an independent researcher to achieve the same results given the same experimental conditions. In machine learning, this often involves having access to the same code, data, and computational environment. The increasing complexity and resource demands of modern ML models, particularly those developed by large tech companies, are making this goal harder to attain.

This approval marks a significant milestone as it is China's first innovative drug developed with AI assistance, demonstrating the accelerating integration of artificial intelligence in pharmaceutical research and development. The remarkably short three-and-a-half-year development timeline from initial discovery to clinical trials highlights the potential of AI to expedite drug creation and bring treatments to market faster. The drug, Istradefylline Hydrochloride, was developed in just three and a half years from initial discovery to the completion of clinical trials, a process significantly accelerated by AI. It has received conditional approval, suggesting further post-market evaluation may be required.

Isar Aerospace successfully launched its two-stage Spectrum rocket into near-Earth orbit on Saturday from the Andøya Space launch site in Norway's Arctic Circle. This marks the first time a privately developed rocket from mainland Europe has achieved orbital insertion. This achievement signifies a major milestone for the European private spaceflight sector, granting the continent independent access to space. It opens a new chapter for European space capabilities and reduces reliance on external launch providers. The 28-meter tall Spectrum rocket carried 5 small satellites and an experimental payload into orbit. The rocket utilizes liquid oxygen and propane for its propulsion, which Isar Aerospace states offers high performance and minimizes environmental impact.
