MLVC is a new learned video codec designed for real-world deployment, addressing the computational efficiency and cross-platform compatibility issues that have limited previous AI-based codecs. It achieves this by transmitting entropy model scale parameters through the hyperprior, enabling both encoding and decoding to run at approximately 100 FPS on consumer NPUs. This development signifies a crucial step towards the practical adoption of AI-based video codecs, potentially leading to more efficient and higher-quality video compression solutions that can rival or surpass traditional codecs like H.264 and H.265 in real-world applications. MLVC overcomes cross-platform incompatibility by explicitly transmitting entropy model scale parameters via the hyperprior, avoiding the need for bit-exact neural network execution across different hardware. Both encoding and decoding achieve around 100 FPS for 360p/540p video on consumer NPUs.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/tanelai · Jul 30, 19:40

**Background**: Traditional video codecs like H.264, H.265, and AV1 are widely used due to their hardware acceleration and efficiency. Learned video codecs, while showing promise in coding efficiency, have been hindered by high computational costs and cross-platform compatibility issues, where small numerical differences can cause decoding failures. Entropy coding is a lossless compression technique that aims to approach the theoretical lower bound of data compression.

This was enabled by engineering innovations including Kimi Delta Attention and Quantile Balancing, alongside efficient runtime optimizations. Kimi K3 demonstrates that open-weight models can compete with top proprietary models, democratizing access to advanced AI capabilities. Its novel architectural components offer new avenues for improving LLM efficiency and performance. Kimi Delta Attention replaces KV cache in many layers with smaller matrices, significantly reducing memory usage for long contexts (e.g., 1M tokens use 27.2 GiB vs. 104.6 GiB). Quantile Balancing ensures even loading across 896 experts per layer by directly computing bias from router score margins, avoiding issues seen in other MoE models.

The training utilized AgentENV, a runtime environment built on Firecracker microVMs for efficient sandbox management during reinforcement learning.
